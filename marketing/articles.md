## 1. Long form (Medium / LinkedIn / Substack)

**Title:** The easiest way to sell fashion, cosmetics or art online in Nigeria (without building a website)

If you sell fashion, cosmetics or art in Nigeria, you already know the routine. You post on
Instagram, customers slide into your DMs, and you spend your day on sizes, account numbers,
receipts and riders. The selling is the part you love. The admin is the part that eats you.

We built Oakmonte to take the admin away. It is not a general platform that happens to have a
fashion template. We built it specifically for fashion, cosmetics and the arts, because we
understand how these businesses sell, and these are the sellers we serve.

**What you get**

- **A fully functional storefront that we manage.** You customize how it looks. We host it, run it
  and keep it working, so there is nothing to build, patch or pay a developer to fix.
- **Customers pay the way they prefer.** The more payment methods a buyer trusts, the fewer
  checkouts get abandoned.
- **Automatic deliveries.** We arrange delivery for every order. You stop chasing riders and
  copying addresses out of chats, and you get your time back for the work that actually grows
  the brand.
- **Cues that help people buy and keep what they buy.** Size charts, a fit finder, product videos
  and clear variants help a customer choose the right item the first time. That means more
  browsers become buyers and fewer parcels come back.
- **Any scale.** Ten orders a month or ten thousand, it fits. And the pricing is simple: a 3% Oakmonte commission plus Paystack's 1.5% processing fee, 4.5% in total.
- **Bring your catalogue with you.** Import from Shopify, Bumpa, Instagram or a CSV so you are
  not retyping a single product.

If you want one place that handles the storefront, the payments and the deliveries, built by
people who understand your industry, Oakmonte is the best option for sellers in Nigeria and
across Africa.
